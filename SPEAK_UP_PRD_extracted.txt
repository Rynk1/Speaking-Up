GHANA CIVIC AWARENESS & CITIZEN REPORTING NETWORK
Full-Stack Product Requirements Document and Google AI Studio Implementation Specification
1. PRODUCT VISION
Build a premium, mobile-first, full-stack civic awareness and citizen reporting platform for Ghana.
The platform enables ordinary citizens — including people with:
few or no social-media followers;
low digital literacy;
limited technical skills;
poor access to formal complaint systems;
limited knowledge of government institutional structures;
limited ability to make their voices heard publicly;
to quickly publish issues happening around them and bring those issues to the attention of:
relevant state institutions;
emergency/security bodies;
regulators;
local authorities;
civil-society organisations;
journalists;
community leaders;
other citizens;
the broader Ghanaian public.
The platform should function socially like a modern social network, but its purpose is fundamentally different.
It is not designed primarily for entertainment, followers or personal popularity.
It is designed to make:
Important citizen-observed problems visible, discoverable, shareable and difficult to ignore.
2. THE CENTRAL PRODUCT IDEA
The core product is:
"A megaphone for citizens who otherwise have no megaphone."
A citizen should be able to open the platform and say:
"This is happening here."
They should be able to:
describe it;
photograph it;
record it;
video it;
locate it;
publish it;
tag the relevant institution;
immediately notify relevant institutional channels;
make the post visible to nearby citizens;
allow others to amplify it;
share it externally to social networks;
build public awareness around the issue.
The platform therefore combines:
Citizen journalism
Civic reporting
Social amplification
Institutional awareness
Geographic issue intelligence.
3. WHAT THIS PLATFORM IS NOT
Do NOT build the product primarily as:
a government complaint portal;
a ticketing system;
a CRM;
a police reporting replacement;
an emergency dispatch service;
a formal legal complaint mechanism;
an adjudication platform;
an institutional case-management system.
The platform does not need to guarantee:
"The institution will investigate your complaint."
Instead it guarantees something fundamentally different:
"Your voice can reach the relevant audience."
The platform helps make the issue visible.
4. THE NEW CORE LOOP
The fundamental product loop is:
CITIZEN SEES SOMETHING
        ↓
CITIZEN POSTS
        ↓
PLATFORM UNDERSTANDS
        ↓
CITIZEN TAGS / PLATFORM SUGGESTS RELEVANT INSTITUTIONS
        ↓
INSTITUTIONAL ALERT
        ↓
PUBLIC DISCOVERY
        ↓
OTHERS CONFIRM / SHARE / ADD EVIDENCE
        ↓
ISSUE GAINS VISIBILITY
        ↓
EXTERNAL SOCIAL AMPLIFICATION
        ↓
MEDIA / CIVIL SOCIETY / PUBLIC ATTENTION
        ↓
INSTITUTION BECOMES AWARE
        ↓
PUBLIC CAN SEE WHETHER THERE HAS BEEN A RESPONSE
The system is therefore fundamentally an:
AWARENESS AND AMPLIFICATION ENGINE.
5. THE PRODUCT'S DISRUPTIVE PROPOSITION
Existing social networks reward people who already have:
followers;
influence;
social connections;
established audiences.
This platform should invert that.
A person with:
0 followers
should still be capable of producing a post that reaches:
the relevant institution;
nearby citizens;
people interested in the issue;
journalists;
civil-society organisations;
public-interest actors;
external social-media audiences.
This is the core differentiation.
6. PRODUCT NAME CONCEPT

Possible positioning:

16. FUNCTIONAL INSTITUTION TAGS
When a citizen tags an institution, the platform should:
publish the post;
associate the post with the institution;
determine available official notification channels;
notify the institution through configured channels where technically permitted;
record that the institution was alerted;
expose the institution's verified profile;
allow the institution to respond publicly.

20. DO NOT PRETEND A SOCIAL-MEDIA MENTION IS AN OFFICIAL COMPLAINT
This distinction must be prominent.
If the platform posts:
@GhanaPoliceService
it must NOT tell the citizen:
"The Police have received your complaint."
Instead:
The post has been publicly associated with the Ghana Police Service.
If an actual supported alert is sent:
Ghana Police Service has been notified through their configured platform channel.
If there is no acknowledgement:
No institutional acknowledgement has been recorded.
This is crucial for trust.

it tags an institution;

37. EMERGENCY POSTS
Emergency posts receive a special presentation.
Examples:
active fire;
serious road accident;
violent incident;
major flooding;
building collapse;
immediate public danger.
Display:
URGENT PUBLIC SAFETY ALERT
and prominently provide official emergency contacts.
Ghana's Ministry of the Interior publishes 112 as the Emergency Response Centre and 191/192/193 for Police, Fire and Ambulance respectively.
The platform must state:
For immediate danger, contact emergency services directly. Posting here does not dispatch emergency services unless an explicitly configured integration exists.
38. EMERGENCY AMPLIFICATION

verified media organisations.
Do not allow emergency ranking to become sensational.
39. PUBLIC SAFETY MODE
For emergency content, the platform can suppress:

location;
official information;

75. INSTITUTIONAL DIRECTORY RESEARCH

The system:

detects approximate location;
suggests the local authority;
optionally suggests another relevant institution.
Citizen taps:
Publish
The post appears publicly.
It says:
Citizen reported
Open drainage hazard near [location]
Below:
Relevant institution
[Verified Institution]
Then:
ALERT INSTITUTION
The platform sends the configured alert.
The citizen sees:
Alert sent
if actually sent.
Others see the post.
Five people tap:
I'm seeing this too
Three add photos.
Someone reposts it to WhatsApp.
Another person shares it to X.
The issue begins trending locally.
The institution sees:
12 citizen posts / confirmations concerning the same location
The institution publishes:
Official response

followers;

technical expertise;
knowledge of the correct ministry;

That is the product.

Build this as a social civic-awareness network, not as a government complaint-management application.
The interface should feel familiar to someone who understands:
Facebook;
X;
TikTok;
WhatsApp;
but the underlying architecture should be purpose-built for:
civic reporting;
public awareness;
institutional visibility;
geographic intelligence;
responsible amplification.
The product should be:
AS SIMPLE AS SOCIAL MEDIA
for citizens,
but
AS STRUCTURED AS AN INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
underneath.
Do not make citizens navigate bureaucracy.
Do not require followers.
Do not require technical knowledge.
Do not require citizens to know which institution is responsible.
Do not require government APIs for the MVP.
Do not fabricate institutional notifications.
Do not fabricate AI analysis.
Do not fabricate responses.
Do not turn every issue into a formal complaint.
Do not create a system designed around closing tickets.
Instead:
Give every Ghanaian a digital megaphone.
When a citizen sees something that matters:
SPEAK UP.
The platform should then help them:
SHOW IT.
LOCATE IT.
TAG WHO NEEDS TO KNOW.
MAKE THE PUBLIC AWARE.
LET OTHERS CONFIRM IT.
AMPLIFY IT.
CONNECT IT TO RELEVANT INSTITUTIONS.
SURFACE EMERGING PATTERNS.
SHOW OFFICIAL RESPONSES WHEN THEY OCCUR.
The system should transform isolated citizen observations into visible, geographically contextualised, institutionally connected public issues.
That is the overarching objective.
Build the architecture, database, APIs, AI services, media pipeline, social layer, institutional directory, notification system, moderation system, analytics and security model around this objective.
Most importantly:
Do not mistake a beautiful frontend prototype for completion.
Every button representing a real capability must be connected to real application logic, persistence, authorization and error handling.
Where an external integration is unavailable, explicitly represent its state rather than simulating success.
The final application must be deployable as a real full-stack MVP and architected so that institutional integrations, WhatsApp/SMS, media partnerships and national civic intelligence can be added without rewriting the core platform.
