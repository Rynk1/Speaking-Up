# Creator Amplification Engine & Referral Tracking Specification

## 1. Level 2 Creator Pack Bundles
Empowers content creators, YouTubers, and civic activists by generating downloadable public-safe story packages:
* Square and vertical visual cards.
* Watermarked audio quote snippets.
* Formatted captions with official institution tags (`@ECG`, `@GhanaPoliceService`).
* Short referral tracking links (`/s/:code`).

## 2. Referral Conversion Analytics
Tracks share creation, link clicks, registration conversions, and subsequent confirmations to reward creators with public platform reputation without compromising citizen privacy.
