# Institutional Registry & Routing Specification

## 1. Registry Architecture
The registry (`institutions` table) catalogs Ghanaian public bodies, security services, utilities, and MMDAs.

## 2. Key Ghanaian Agencies
* **Electricity Company of Ghana (ECG)**: Power distribution in Southern Ghana.
* **Northern Electricity Distribution Co. (NEDCo)**: Power distribution in Northern Ghana.
* **Ghana Water Company Limited (GWCL)**: Urban water supply and mains repair.
* **Ghana Police Service (GPS)**: Security, crime reporting, traffic management.
* **National Disaster Management Org. (NADMO)**: Flood relief, natural disasters, emergency response.
* **Ghana National Fire Service (GNFS)**: Fire outbreaks, hazardous material response.
* **Metropolitan/Municipal/District Assemblies (MMDAs)**: Local sanitation, roads, public infrastructure across 261 districts.
