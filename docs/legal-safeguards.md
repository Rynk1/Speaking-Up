# Legal Safeguards & Statutory Compliance in Ghana

## 1. Statutory Alignments
* **1992 Constitution of Ghana**: Article 21(1)(a) & (f) freedom of speech and right to information.
* **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**: Enforced via P³RE PII redaction pipeline.
* **Cybersecurity Act, 2020 (Act 1038)**: Incident reporting and illegal material detection.
* **Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772)**: Intermediary service provider notice-and-takedown obligations.

## 2. Matters Mandatory for Ghana-Qualified Legal Counsel Review
* Intermediary safe harbor terms for citizen-contributed allegations.
* Statutory Whistleblower Protection Act, 2006 (Act 720) compliance for P³RE submissions.
* Defamation liability boundary management.
